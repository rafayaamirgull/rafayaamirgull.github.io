I am a robotics and computer vision software engineer and an M.Sc. Mobile Robotics student at the University of Bonn. I work as a student research assistant at DeepMI, DZNE, and the Humanoid Robots Lab, developing neuroimaging software and robot vision systems.

Built a perception-action loop that recovers an eye-in-hand camera viewpoint from a target RGB image. Integrated DINOv3/SIFT image matching, DepthAnything depth estimation, FoundationPose 6D object pose estimation, and CuRobo inverse kinematics and collision-free motion planning. In six-object scenes, loop runtimes were 35–50 s in simulation and 62–87 s on hardware, depending on object shape and texture.
